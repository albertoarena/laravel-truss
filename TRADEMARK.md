# Trademark and brand policy

Laravel Truss is free software, released under the [MIT licence](LICENSE). This page
is about the things the licence does not cover: the project name and the brand
around it.

Nothing on this page takes away any right the MIT licence gives you over the code.

## What the MIT licence covers

The MIT licence covers the **source code** of Laravel Truss. You may use it, copy it,
change it, merge it into your own work, publish it, and sell it, for free, as long as
you keep the copyright notice and the licence text with it.

## What this policy covers

The MIT licence is a copyright licence. It does not grant any right to use the
project's name or brand. Those are separate, and they are:

- The names **Laravel Truss** and **Truss**, when used for database structure
  tooling.
- The tagline **`Structure only, never data.`**
- The logo and cover art in [`art/`](art/), in all colour variants.
- The design, layout and written copy of [trussphp.com](https://trussphp.com).

These identify this project. They are how people know that a thing came from here,
and they are not part of the MIT grant.

## What you can do without asking

You do not need permission for any of this, and you are welcome to it:

- Say that your project uses, supports, works with, or integrates with Laravel Truss.
- Link to the repository, to [trussphp.com](https://trussphp.com), or to the demo.
- Write articles, tutorials, videos, courses or talks about Laravel Truss, including
  paid ones, and use the name to say what they are about.
- Use the unmodified cover art from [`art/`](art/) to illustrate an article or a talk
  about Laravel Truss, with a link back to the project.
- Publish a package that extends Laravel Truss, named in the "for" or "with" form:
  `truss-postgres-driver`, `laravel-truss-themes`, `nova-truss-panel`. The name has to
  read as an add-on to this project, not as this project.
- **Fork the repository on GitHub to contribute.** Nothing on this page applies to
  that. See "Contributing" below.
- Fork the code and change it for your own use, as the licence allows.

## What needs permission

Please do not do any of this without asking first:

- Name **your own** product, package, domain, subdomain, app, social account or
  company in a way that could be read as this project or as an official version of it.
  That includes `laravel-truss-*`, `truss-*` and `*-truss` used as the product's own
  name, and it includes hosted apps at any address containing "laravel-truss" or
  "trussphp". **A GitHub fork made to contribute is not covered**: it keeps this
  project's name, and it should.
- Use the tagline `Structure only, never data.` on your own product, in your own
  marketing, or as your own line. It is the line this project is known by.
- Use the logo or cover art as the icon, logo, favicon or hero image of your product,
  or change it and use the result.
- Say or imply that your work is official, endorsed, approved, certified, affiliated
  with, or built by the Laravel Truss project, when it is not.
- Register a trademark, domain or app-store name containing "Laravel Truss", "Truss"
  in a database tooling context, or a name close enough to be confused with them.
- Sell merchandise carrying the name, the logo or the tagline.

## Rebuilds and reimplementations

You are free to build your own database structure viewer. The idea is not owned by
anyone, and a good one is welcome.

If you build your own, give it your own name, your own tagline and your own look. Do
not launch it as a "Laravel Truss clone", do not put "laravel-truss" in its address,
and do not carry over the tagline or the art. You may of course say, in the
description, that Laravel Truss inspired it, and a link back is appreciated.

The reason is not ownership of the idea. It is that a person who lands on your page
and thinks it is this project will ask this project for support, will report its bugs
here, and will judge Laravel Truss by software that Laravel Truss did not write.

## Contributing: nothing here applies

If you are forking the repository to fix a bug, add a feature or open a pull request,
**stop reading. This page is not about you.**

Fork it, keep the name, keep `composer.json` exactly as it is, push your branch, open
the PR. Your fork is meant to look like Laravel Truss, because it is Laravel Truss.
That is how every contribution to this project has arrived, and it needs no
permission from anyone. See [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).

The section below is about something different: publishing your version as a separate
project that people install instead of this one.

## Publishing your own distribution

This is a hard fork, not a contribution: you have decided to maintain and distribute
your own version, under your own name, for other people to install. The MIT licence
lets you, and there is nothing wrong with it.

When you do:

- Give the distribution a different name, and change the package name in
  `composer.json` so it is a separate package on Packagist.
- Keep the copyright notice and the licence text, as the licence requires.
- Say in the README that it is a fork of Laravel Truss, and link back here.
- Do not use the Laravel Truss logo or tagline as the fork's own brand.

**The test is whether someone can install your version by mistake**, thinking it is
this one. A pull request cannot. A package on Packagist can.
